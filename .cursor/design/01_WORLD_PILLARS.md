# World Pillars

The following pillars define the foundation of the Undruinocpp universe.

Every level, prop, character, faction, mechanic, and story should reinforce these ideas.

If a feature contradicts one of these pillars, it should be reconsidered.

---

## Pillar 1 — Humanity Lives in Exile

Humanity no longer possesses a home.

The flotilla is not transportation.

It is civilization.

Entire generations have lived and died aboard ships.

Many have never experienced rain, oceans, forests, or open skies.

The fleet is humanity's last surviving nation.

---

## Pillar 2 — Survival Through Ingenuity

Humanity does not survive because it possesses superior technology.

It survives because it can repair, adapt, improvise, and build.

Nothing useful is discarded.

Everything has another purpose.

Every object has lived many lives.

Ingenuity is humanity's greatest natural resource.

---

## Pillar 3 — Every Object Has History

Nothing aboard the fleet is pristine.

Everything has been repaired.

Modified.

Repurposed.

Inherited.

A machine is not merely equipment.

It is a record of the generations who kept it alive.

Wear is evidence of survival.

Repairs are beautiful.

---

## Pillar 4 — Humanity Has Lost Its Identity

The fleet remembers fragments.

Languages have changed.

Traditions have shifted.

Records have disappeared.

Engineering procedures became rituals.

Stories became history.

History became myth.

Humanity remembers where it came from only imperfectly.

---

## Pillar 5 — Hope Divides Humanity

Everyone wants humanity to survive.

Few agree on how.

Some search for a new world.

Some believe home was abandoned too quickly.

Some believe no world will matter until humanity earns acceptance again.

The great conflicts of the fleet are not born from hatred.

They are born from different visions of hope.

---

## Pillar 6 — Civilization Is Maintenance

The greatest heroes are not always soldiers.

They are engineers.

Teachers.

Doctors.

Mechanics.

Archivists.

Farmers.

The people who ensure another generation survives.

Keeping civilization alive is humanity's greatest achievement.

---

## Pillar 7 — The Fleet Is Alive

The ships are not merely vehicles.

They are homes.

Cities.

Factories.

Museums.

Temples.

Schools.

Generations have shaped them.

The fleet grows and changes alongside the people who inhabit it.

---

## Pillar 8 — There Is No Single Humanity

Humanity is too vast to be understood through a single philosophy.

Every faction believes it is acting in humanity's best interest.

Every philosophy emerges from genuine hope.

Every philosophy sacrifices something in pursuit of that hope.

Every philosophy contains blind spots.

The fleet is not divided because some people are good and others are evil.

It is divided because every generation asks the same impossible question:

"What does humanity need to survive?"

The answers are different.

All of them deserve to be understood before they are judged.
